# S6 - Anglais — CE Listening 5 and 6 Exam Practice.docx

## Summary

Two graded CE listening comprehension exam exercises for an S6 Business English course. LC1 covers gender pay differences (True/False format, 8 pts); LC2 covers remote working in Vermont during the pandemic (open questions, 12 pts). Student scored 6/8 on LC1 and 6/12 on LC2.

## Fields/API

**name**: LC1 — Gender Pay Gap
**format**: True/False, 8 questions × 1 pt = 8 pts
**student_score**: 6/8
**recording_plays**: 2
**prep_time**: 1 minute
**key_statements**: - 1. Women as likely as men to negotiate salaries — F
- 2. People unaware of colleagues' salaries — T
- 3. Employees get substantial pay rise when changing jobs — T
- 4. Negotiating pay can have negative consequences for women — T
- 5. New policies aim to eliminate in-company pay negotiations — ambiguous (T/F both marked)
- 6. Robin Ryan says companies generally want women promoted — ambiguous (T/F both marked)
- 7. HR consultants encourage publishing salary info — T (T/F both marked, likely error)
- 8. Online pay transparency attracts and retains employees — T
**name**: LC2 — Remote Working / Vermont
**format**: Open questions, 12 pts total
**student_score**: 6/12
**recording_plays**: 2
**prep_time**: not stated
**questions_and_answers**: - Q1 (1pt): Why did Allred relocate to Vermont? — Husband had a job there. [1pt awarded]
- Q2 (2pts): Two advantages of Burlington area for Allred? — Work flexibility/mobility. [0.5pt awarded]
- Q3 (1pt): Principal field attracting remote workers to Vermont? — Tech sector. [answer given: 'next to nature' — incorrect]
- Q4 (1pt): Impact of remote working on IBM hiring? — Opened a new pool of workers. [1pt awarded]
- Q5 (2pts): Vermont incentive program name + 2019 participants? — Financial relocation incentive; 140 people. [1+1 awarded]
- Q6 (3pts): Two future trends for Vermont per Jeff Carr? — Many movers plan to stay; people will relocate post-vaccination. [1pt awarded, partial]
- Q7 (2pts): Where does Allred plan to move and why? — Colorado or Montana; to continue remote-work lifestyle. [0.5pt awarded]

## Constraints

- Grammar and spelling not graded on LC2.
- Each recording heard exactly twice.
- LC1: 1 point per correct T/F answer.
- LC2: Variable point weighting per question (1, 2, 1, 1, 2, 3, 2).
- Questions 5 and 6 of LC1 show double marks (T and F both ticked) — likely annotation errors by the student.

## Examples

- LC1 Q4 (T): 'Negotiating pay levels can sometimes have negative consequences for women.' — confirmed true by broadcast.
- LC1 Q8 (T): 'Online pay transparency can result in people wanting to join and stay in a company.' — confirmed true.
- LC2 Q4: IBM expanded its hiring pool by recruiting remote workers regardless of geographic location.
- LC2 Q5: Vermont's relocation incentive program drew 140 participants in 2019.
