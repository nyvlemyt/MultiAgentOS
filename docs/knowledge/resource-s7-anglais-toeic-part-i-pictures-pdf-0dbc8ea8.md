# S7 - anglais — TOEIC Part I - Pictures.pdf

## Summary

TOEIC Listening Part 1 consists of 6 photographs; for each, 4 spoken sentences (A–D) are read and the test-taker selects the one that best describes the photo. No written descriptions are provided.

## Fields/API

**format**: **questions**: 6
**options_per_question**: 4
**intro_time**: 1 min 25 sec (use to preview photos)
**interval_between_options**: 5 seconds
**time_after_each_answer**: 3 sec fill sheet + 2 sec preview next photo
**photo_types**: **people**: One or multiple people — describe gender, clothes, occupation, body-part actions (eye, hand, leg)
**objects_and_landscape**: Focus on object name, position (on/in/next to/behind), status (open/closed/full/empty), and location context
**descriptive_structures**: **people_photos**: Subject + present continuous verb (+ object/location): e.g. 'The woman is shaking hands with a colleague.'
**object_photos**: Object + position/state: e.g. 'The chairs are arranged around the table.' / 'The store is closed.'
**common_distractor_types**: **similar_sounding_words**: Words that sound alike but differ in meaning (e.g. 'knitting' vs 'sitting') — the visible element may be correct but the verb is wrong.
**wrong_context**: Correct nouns from the photo used with an incorrect action (e.g. 'He is typing on the keyboard' when the man is not touching it).
**wrong_preposition**: Correct objects but wrong spatial relation (e.g. 'cars on the bridge' when they are under it).
**key_listening_targets**: **SVO_pattern**: Subject–Verb–Object is the dominant sentence structure; isolate each element and verify against the photo.
**verbs**: Highest-value element — a single wrong verb eliminates the option.
**prepositions**: Position/direction words (on, under, next to, across from, behind, in the middle of) are a frequent trick.
**synonym_examples**: - shaking hands / greeting each other
- taking notes / writing something down / writing on the paper
- making a call / hanging up the phone / talking on the phone
- having a meeting / sitting opposite each other / sitting across from each other

## Constraints

- No written answer choices — audio only; must recall all four options while listening.
- Cannot replay audio; answer must be committed within ~3 seconds of the last option.
- Distractors deliberately reuse nouns visible in the photo — correct noun ≠ correct answer.
- Similar-sounding words (minimal pairs) are intentional traps; phonetic accuracy is required.

## Examples

**distractor_type**: similar sound
**incorrect**: The woman is knitting.
**correct**: The woman is sitting.
**note**: 'knit' and 'sit' sound similar; the photo shows sitting, not knitting.
**distractor_type**: wrong context
**incorrect**: He is typing on the keyboard.
**correct**: He is standing next to the keyboard.
**note**: Keyboard is visible but the man is not using it.
**distractor_type**: wrong preposition
**incorrect**: There are many cars on the bridge.
**correct**: There are many cars under the bridge.
**note**: Preposition 'on' vs 'under' changes meaning entirely.
