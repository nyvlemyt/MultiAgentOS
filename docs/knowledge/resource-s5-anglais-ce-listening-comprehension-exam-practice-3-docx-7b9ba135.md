# S5 - Anglais — CE Listening Comprehension Exam Practice 3.docx

## Summary

Feuille d'examen blanc de compréhension orale en anglais des affaires (S5). Deux exercices audio distincts : LC1 sur les écarts de salaire hommes/femmes (8 questions Vrai/Faux, 8 pts) et LC2 sur le télétravail pendant la pandémie (7 questions ouvertes, 12 pts). Chaque enregistrement est écouté deux fois ; 1 minute de lecture des questions est accordée avant chaque écoute.

## Fields/API

**name**: LC1 — Gender Pay Gap
**type**: True/False
**points**: 8 (1 pt/question)
**questions**: - 1. Women are as likely as men to negotiate salaries.
- 2. Colleagues' salaries are often unknown today.
- 3. Many employees get a large pay rise when changing jobs.
- 4. Pay negotiation can have negative consequences for women.
- 5. New government policies aim to eliminate in-company pay negotiations.
- 6. Robin Ryan says companies generally want women promoted.
- 7. HR consultants encourage companies to publish salary info.
- 8. Online pay transparency helps attract and retain staff.
**name**: LC2 — Remote Working & Vermont
**type**: Open questions (grammar/spelling not graded)
**points**: 12 total
**questions**: - Q1 (1pt): Principal reason for Allred's relocation to Vermont.
- Q2 (2pts): Two advantages Allred appreciates about the Burlington area.
- Q3 (1pt): Principal field attracting remote workers to Vermont.
- Q4 (1pt): Impact of remote working on IBM's hiring process.
- Q5 (2pts): One Vermont relocation incentive program + number of participants in 2019.
- Q6 (3pts): Two future demographic trends for Vermont predicted by Jeff Carr.
- Q7 (2pts): Where Allred plans to move next and why.

## Constraints

- Each recording is heard exactly twice.
- 1 minute of reading time is given before each listening segment.
- LC1: grammar and spelling irrelevant (X in T or F column).
- LC2: grammar and spelling are explicitly not penalised.
- Total exam score: 20 points (LC1 = 8 pts, LC2 = 12 pts).

## Examples

- LC1 sample: Statement 4 — 'Negotiating pay levels can sometimes have negative consequences for women.' → mark T or F after listening.
- LC2 sample: Q5 asks for both the program name AND the 2019 participant count (2 separate marks).
