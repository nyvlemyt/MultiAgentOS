## Constraints

- Verbs are in past tense (simple past) — standard convention for resume bullet points describing past roles.
- Use present tense equivalents when describing current responsibilities.
- Each bullet point should begin with one action verb to maintain parallel structure.
- Attributes (adjectives/nouns) are distinct from verbs — use them in profile summaries or cover letter prose, not as bullet starters.

## Examples

- Led a cross-functional team of 8 engineers to deliver the platform three weeks ahead of schedule.
- Spearheaded the migration from legacy infrastructure, reducing operational costs by 30%.
- Mentored junior developers on best practices, improving code review turnaround by 40%.
- Collaborated with stakeholders to gather requirements and translated them into actionable technical specs.
- Cover letter attribute usage: 'As a quick learner with a diligent work ethic, I thrive in fast-paced environments.'
