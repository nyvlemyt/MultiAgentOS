## Examples

- Reciprocal tariff concept (item 4): US mirrors the tariff rate another country imposes on US exports.
- Piggy bank metaphor (item 2): Trump's rhetorical framing of perceived US trade losses to foreign partners.
- Household cost figure (item 9): $3,000/year estimated annual burden on American households cited in the audio.
- Canadian cheese example (item 5): illustrative case of a foreign tariff applied to a US agricultural export.
