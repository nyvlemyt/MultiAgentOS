# Facebook to raise minimum wage to $15 an hour for contractors and vendors By Sam Thielman, [www.theguardian.com](http://www.theguardian.com), May 13, 2015

## Summary

DE Final Exam practice document on job market skills in English, comprising three graded reading comprehension exercises (Facebook $15 minimum wage, Google Q1 2015 results, Verbling startup pitch) and a formal cover letter writing task targeting an IT internship at Zimmer Biomet. Total: 40 points.

## Fields/API

**Reading Comprehension 1 — Facebook/minimum wage (8 pts)**: True/False exercise on Facebook raising contractor minimum wage to $15/hr, announced May 2015 by COO Sheryl Sandberg. Motivations cited: gender equity (women ≈ 2/3 of minimum-wage workers), workforce productivity. Answer key: F, T, F, F, T, F, T, F.
**Reading Comprehension 2 — Google Q1 2015 results (8 pts)**: Vocabulary-in-context exercise. Eight bolded terms to match: offset (counterbalance), forecast (prediction), brace for (prepare for), run up (generate), relief rally (expected bad outcome does not occur), net (after deductions), tweaked (slightly modified), share (part of capital stock).
**Reading Comprehension 3 — Verbling startup pitch (4 pts)**: Open-ended full-sentence questions on a language-learning video platform seeking funding. Key points: $32B market + instant matching (founders); replicability risk (Warren Lee); competition from Livemocha with $19M raised (Jon Chait); need for quality control and objective skill assessment (Jon Sakoda).
**Writing — Cover Letter (20 pts)**: Formal email cover letter for IT Summer Intern 2025 at Zimmer Biomet. Graded: structure 4 pts (intro/body/conclusion, formal salutations), content 8 pts (role identification, interest, experience-to-JD match with examples, soft skills, CV reference), language 8 pts (grammar, syntax, vocabulary, formal register, punctuation).

## Constraints

- Total: 40 pts (20 reading + 20 writing).
- RC1: 1 pt each, T/F only — no partial credit.
- RC2: 1 pt each, single best match from three options.
- RC3: 0.5 pt content + 0.5 pt language quality per question; full sentences required; copying text verbatim scores 0.
- Cover letter: experience may be adapted to fit the posting; formal conventions mandatory.
- RC2 vocabulary answers: offset=b, forecast=c, brace for=b, run up=b, relief rally=c, net=a, tweaked=b, share=c.

## Examples

- RC1 Q5 (TRUE): Sandberg wrote that women, comprising ~2/3 of minimum-wage workers nationally, are particularly affected by wage adjustments.
- RC1 Q6 (FALSE): Sandberg's Lean In foundation initially posted an unpaid-intern listing in 2013, contradicting a perfect fairness record.
- RC2 'tweaked': b) slightly modified — Google made a minor algorithm change favouring mobile-friendly sites.
- RC3 Q3 model answer: Jon Chait's biggest obstacle for Verbling is intense competition; Livemocha alone has raised $19M and attracted over 11 million users, making it unclear how Verbling differentiates itself.
- Cover letter target: IT Intern, Summer 2025, Zimmer Biomet — emphasise SQL/programming/network skills, analytical problem-solving, and alignment with company values.
